## 📋 Tasks & Responsibilities
Every intern is expected to fulfill the following 8 core responsibilities:
1. **Understand requirements:** Understand the project requirements and objectives clearly.
2. **Clean & scalable engineering:** Design and develop clean, efficient, and scalable code.
3. **Standards & patterns:** Follow Algoryx coding standards, architecture patterns, and naming conventions.
4. **Quality & testing:** Test your code thoroughly and ensure production quality.
5. **Deployment:** Deploy the application and provide the **Live URL**.
6. **Documentation & repository:** Document your work thoroughly and maintain a clean, professional GitHub repository.
7. **Professional LinkedIn post:** Create a professional LinkedIn post about your project (**mandatory**).
8. **Timely submission:** Submit all deliverables as per the defined guidelines and deadlines.
